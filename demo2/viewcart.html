Frequently asked questions

Is a latex mattress good for back pain?

Latex is naturally firm and responsive, which helps keep the spine aligned and reduces pressure points. Many people with back discomfort prefer it.

Can a latex mattress cause allergies?

Organic latex naturally resists dust mites and mould. People with a known latex allergy should check with their doctor first.

What is the difference between latex and memory foam?

Memory foam contours closely to the body, while latex is bouncier and more breathable. Both can offer good spinal support.

How should I care for the mattress?
