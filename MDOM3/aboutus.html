About Us

Our Story

MDOM was started in the year 2010.

As we were already a healthcare Backoffice Service Provider for over a decade, one of our major clients asked us to find a HIPAA compliant email service that was affordable for their budget and reliable for the business communication of their 2,000+ clients. When we started exploring for it, we were surprised we couldn't find any good options. That's when the idea for our Encrypted Email Service germinated.

Over the past 15 years, our HIPAA compliant email service has been created, improved, and evolved to match today’s security expectations.We have significantly upgraded our security and HIPAA compliances, ensuring that no potential breach point was ever left unaddressed.
