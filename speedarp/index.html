App Details

Why users choose Speedarp

  • First-of-its-kind family networking:

    Speedarp blends the best of social networking with dedicated privacy, designed exclusively for families and relatives.

  • Family Tree without DNA:

    Create your own family tree easily — no DNA data required. Even add the loved ones who passed away (remembered) to your family tree.

  • Customized Family Events

    Celebrate milestones like anniversaries, housewarmings, baby showers, retirement parties, and more with personalized event creation. It is also possible to remain updated on upcoming events and track the previous events.

  • Occupational Compass:

    Discover the professional diversity among your family members, cousins and relatives.

  • Private Image Sharing

    Photos and memories remain within your trusted circle — shared only among family members, cousins, and relatives.

  • Growing Community

    Already trusted by 100+ families on Play Store, Speedarp is building a vibrant network of legacy keepers.
