Content for Websites

We at Speedarp offer content that not only keeps your website shining, but also keeps reminding your customers regarding the potential your business can offer to them. Technical Documentation

As the dependency on technological infrastructure and devices is increasing, it has become a regulatory requirement for maintaining a documented record of all the technological systems, devices and operations. Following are the areas Speedarp can assist businesses with.

Software Documentation

This type of documentation is related to software applications that are either used or being developed by the LLCs. This comprises of technical specification, user manuals, training materials, source code documentation and others. Having an updated documentation with respect to the software will help the organizations in multiple ways.

Data Management Procedures

This is an important area for the business organizations as it documents the manner in which the organization would be safeguarding and managing the data of its customers, employees and other key stakeholders. Data management policies in this regard are data backup procedures, data storage policies, and related data roles. It is not only important to align the data management procedures with compliance requirements but also have a complete and accurate documentation supporting it.

Network and System Documentation

Networks play the role of blood vessels for the organizations as they help the organizations manage the flow of data in and out of the organization in an organized, controlled and secure manner. Speedarp understands the integrated functionality provided by networking, hardware and software components. We excel in designing network diagrams which helps in understanding the organization’s networks in a better manner.

IT Security Policies

Small businesses have become easier victims for the cyber predators as small businesses have constrained resources and skills to shield them against the security attacks. Considering the challenging landscape of small businesses, Speedarp offers thorough documentation of the IS (information security) policies as per the requirements of the business. Detailed documentation of security procedures will also help in identifying ways to strengthening the security procedures.

Speedarp will create social media content that is relevant, consistent and offers a visual appeal. Speedarp realizes the need for having a content that engages the customers rather than repels them. Storytelling through content is one of the competences being offered by Speedarp.
